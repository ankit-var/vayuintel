## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, authoritative geospatial and environmental intelligence interface tailored to Indian civic contexts, atmospheric scientists, and municipal planners. The aesthetic marries technical credibility with civic accessibility, balancing rigorous scientific data presentation with immediate, actionable public health legibility.

### Design Movement: Modern Scientific Clarity
The design style integrates contemporary Scandinavian data minimalism with institutional reliability. Key tenets include:
- **Calm, High-Information Surface Architecture:** Prioritizing high signal-to-noise ratios via subdued neutral canvases that allow sensor overlays and geospatial maps to command attention.
- **Micro-Structured Enclosures:** High-density metrics, sensor health telemetry, and uncertainty bounds organized within modular, cleanly outlined containers.
- **Institutional Weight with Modern Fluency:** Combining the grounded authority of deep botanical greens with technical teal accents to signal ecological grounding and predictive algorithmic capability.
- **Uncompromising Metric Integrity:** Strict adherence to regulatory compliance standards (Central Pollution Control Board - CPCB) through unambiguous, non-negotiable color protocols.

## Colors

The palette balances serene environmental ground tones with an unyielding, standardized functional scale for air quality telemetry and geospatial analysis.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50) delivers an airy, warm off-white foundation that eliminates ocular fatigue during prolonged analytical workflows.
- **Sub-Surface & Wells:** `#F1F5F9` (Slate 100) provides structural containment for filter bars, map control shelves, and tabular headers.
- **Surface Elevation (Cards):** `#FFFFFF` pure white surfaces establish visual priority against the slate background.
- **Border Gradients & Dividers:** `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200) serves as the primary structural border for crisp spatial delineations, with `#CBD5E1` (Slate 300) reserved for active and hovered boundaries.

### Brand Accent Infrastructure
- **Primary Brand (Deep Forest):** `#064E3B` anchors navigational frameworks, primary metric headers, and institutional verification badges.
- **Primary Interactive (Forest Mid):** `#047857` powers primary actionable buttons, selected navigation states, and baseline environmental indicators.
- **Secondary Accent (Deep Teal):** `#0D9488` highlights predictive ML tracks, atmospheric modeling vectors, and interactive GIS layers.
- **Tertiary Accent (Vibrant Teal):** `#14B8A6` powers micro-indicators, telemetry pulse points, active live streams, and temporal slider highlights.

### Strict CPCB AQI Semantic Scale
All AQI visualizations, contour heatmaps, and metric readouts must strictly conform to the national regulatory CPCB thresholds:
- **Good (0–50):** `#10B981` (Emerald). Associated surface tint: `#ECFDF5`. Text contrast: `#065F46`.
- **Satisfactory (51–100):** `#22C55E` (Green). Associated surface tint: `#F0FDF4`. Text contrast: `#166534`.
- **Moderate (101–200):** `#F59E0B` (Amber). Associated surface tint: `#FFFBEB`. Text contrast: `#92400E`.
- **Poor (201–300):** `#F97316` (Orange). Associated surface tint: `#FFF7ED`. Text contrast: `#9A3412`.
- **Very Poor (301–400):** `#EF4444` (Red). Associated surface tint: `#FEF2F2`. Text contrast: `#991B1B`.
- **Severe (401–500+):** `#881337` (Maroon / Burgundy). Associated surface tint: `#FFF1F2`. Text contrast: `#4C0519`.

### Uncertainty & Data Provenance Palette
To ensure honest reporting of sensor drift, satellite backfill, and ML interpolation:
- **High Confidence / Reference Grade (CAAQMS):** Solid fills using the primary scale.
- **Moderate Confidence / Calibrated Low-Cost Sensor:** Semi-transparent fills (70%) with a micro-stippled border pattern.
- **Modeled / High Uncertainty (ML Geospatial Infill):** Diagonal striping overlays using `#94A3B8` (Slate 400) at 30% alpha over base AQI hues.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs human-centered clarity with technical computing precision:

- **Display & Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for its geometric structure softened by humane optical terminals. It gives regional names (e.g., *Anand Vihar, New Delhi*), public advisories, and macro-metrics presence without appearing overly institutional or aggressive.
- **Narrative & UI Controls (Inter):** Serves as the core workhorse. Neutral, perfectly balanced tall x-height, and superior rendering across variable Indian vernacular character transliterations and dense analytical tables.
- **Technical & Geospatial Data (JetBrains Mono):** Dedicated to raw telemetry (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, SO2, CO, Ozone), latitude/longitude coordinates, timestamps, confidence interval percentages, and device telemetry hardware IDs. This ensures tabular numbers line up vertically during live data updates without jitter.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an uncompromising 4px sub-grid mapped to an 8px base rhythmic cadence. The dashboard employs a fluid-responsive workbench architecture tailored for cross-sectional visualization.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above):** 12-column layout. Gutter width `1.5rem` (24px), page margin `2rem` (32px). Max-width container is clamped to `1600px` for ultra-wide command center screens, keeping maps and graphs within peripheral reach.
- **Tablet / Laptop (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout. Gutter width `1rem` (16px), page margin `1.5rem` (24px). Sidebar collapses to a floating docked rail; map-to-metrics ratio rebalances from split-screen to stacked viewport cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout. Gutter width `0.75rem` (12px), canvas margin `1rem` (16px). Geospatial views transition into full-width cards with swipeable bottom sheets for ward-level drilldowns.

### Layout Mechanics
- **The Telemetry Deck:** Top-level metrics use horizontal scroll containers with snap-points on mobile devices, transitioning to fixed CSS auto-fit grid tiles (`minmax(240px, 1fr)`) on desktop.
- **Split-View Canvas:** Geospatial Map vs. Analytics Split utilizes a dynamic 60/40 ratio on desktop, allowing synchronous coordinate panning while maintaining visible station comparison tables.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve scientific precision, this design system eschews heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp boundary delineation and atmospheric ambient layers.

### Low-Contrast Outlines & Tonal Grounding
Surface separation relies primarily on a `#E2E8F0` border paired with delicate, tinted ambient shadows. This prevents UI elements from floating unpredictably over complex GIS map layers.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** `#F8FAFC`, flat. Background for app layouts, toolbars, and inactive panels.
- **Level 1 (Card Default / Static Container):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`. Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 1px 2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`. Used for metric cards, data grids, and telemetry cards.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Card / Active Layer Control):** `#FFFFFF` fill with a `1px` border of `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0px 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0px 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Elevates active station cards and hovered sensor clusters.
- **Level 3 (Floating HUD / Geospatial Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` with `95%` opacity and an `8px` backdrop-blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`). Border: `1px` solid `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`. Shadow: `0px 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`. Reserved for map floating controllers, time-series scrubber bars, and active tooltips.
- **Level 4 (Modal / Citizen Advisory Panels):** `#FFFFFF` fill with shadow: `0px 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0px 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances precision with approachability by pairing structured containers with generous corner radii:

- **Core Containers & Dashboard Cards (`rounded-2xl` / 16px):** Primary analytical modules, time-series visualization panels, and map bounding boxes feature soft, approachable edges that prevent visual harshness in data-dense layouts.
- **Inner Controls & Secondary Surfaces (`rounded-xl` / 12px):** Sub-cards, modal dialogue sheets, sensor calibration panels, and floating map widgets.
- **Form Controls & Actionables (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** Dropdown triggers, text search inputs, date-range pickers, and primary buttons.
- **Pills & Status Micro-Badges (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** CPCB status indicators, uncertainty tags, live sensor telemetry alerts, and tabular ward rank tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Institutional):** Solid `#064E3B` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-medium`, 8px corner radius. On hover: shifts to `#047857` with transition duration of `150ms`. Focus-visible ring: `2px` offset, `#0D9488`.
- **Secondary Action (Analytical):** `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` border of `#E2E8F0`, `#064E3B` text. On hover: `#F8FAFC` background with `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Ghost / Map Control Buttons:** Transparent background, `#475569` text and icon fill. On hover: `#F1F5F9`.

### Chips & CPCB Status Badges
- **AQI Value Badges:** Minimum height of 28px, rounded-full. Constructed using the semantic AQI background tint at 15% opacity, `1px` solid border using the 40% semantic color tone, and the dark high-contrast semantic label text. Contains a 6px solid circular indicator matching the base CPCB hex.
- **Uncertainty & Provenance Badges:** Outlined in `#E2E8F0`, with `#F8FAFC` fill and `#475569` text. Prefixed with a mono confidence factor (e.g., `±4.2% CI · CAAQMS Ref`).

### Cards & Telemetry Containers
- Metric modules must have an unambiguous visual hierarchy:
  1. Top metadata strip containing the pollutant label (`JetBrains Mono`, `label-sm`), sensor hardware state, and spatial provenance tag.
  2. Hero scalar number (`JetBrains Mono`, `telemetry-num`) paired with immediate unit notation (`µg/m³`).
  3. Contextual comparison tag showing 24-hour moving delta with an directional icon.
  4. Ambient mini sparkline at the base with baseline regulatory standard threshold indicated via an overlay dashed line.

### Lists & Geospatial Sensor Feeds
- Row heights clamped at 48px to preserve high information density.
- Border-bottom at `1px` solid `#F1F5F9`.
- Hover trigger applies `#F8FAFC` across the entire row item with zero horizontal expansion.
- Right-aligned telemetry values rendered in `JetBrains Mono` with fixed widths to prevent visual misalignment during dynamic refresh cycles.

### Input Fields & Filter Controllers
- **Search & Filter Controls:** White `#FFFFFF` background with `1px` border `#E2E8F0`, `rounded-lg`, height `40px`. Padding: `0 12px`. Placeholder text: `#94A3B8`.
- **Active State:** Border transitions to `#0D9488` with a subtle `3px` focus ring: `rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.15)`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Ward-Level Air Dispersion Legend:** Compact horizontal segmented bar strictly ordered according to the 6 CPCB color bands, featuring interactive tooltips displaying absolute numerical thresholds and corresponding citizen health guidance.
- **Temporal Scrubbing Slider (Predictive Timeline):** Track height 6px, filled in `#0D9488`. Thumb styled as an elevated white circular node (`18px`) bounded by a 2px `#064E3B` rim, with an attached floating hover badge displaying predictive model uncertainty bounds.
